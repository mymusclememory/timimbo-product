One system for every Amazon placement

The guide turns NOW's brand into rules a team can follow at speed: one color system per layout, a fixed tagline, two-weight headlines, and components that carry their own contrast notes. This page is the Figma file rebuilt in code. Everything below is live: pick a color system, a gradient or a product colorway and the components follow.

The problem

No cohesive identity on Amazon

NOW's Amazon presence never had one cohesive look behind the scenes. Each category told its story differently. I used consumer data to shape a design system built to convert and to tie every NOW category together.

What I built

Rules, tokens and components in Figma

Eight color systems, five gradients, a purple scale and product colorways as tokens; a Nunito type scale; and components from CTA buttons to full A+ modules, each with usage notes written into Figma.

Impact

Faster to market, more consistent listings

Faster A+ builds, fewer review rounds and more consistent listings, getting products to market quicker with higher consumer engagement.

The 7 rules

The short list every designer checks before a piece ships.

  1. AI-generated content comes from NOW. Any AI-generated content is produced internally by NOW, following approved guidelines, so the brand always appears authentic.
  2. Never alter the product label. Use NOW's approved labels exactly as supplied. No relabeling, recoloring, warping or covering label text for hero imagery. Scale proportionally only.
  3. Color system per layout. Never mix more than 3 color systems.
  4. The tagline is always stacked: QUALITY OBSESSED. (Nunito Black) over PRICED LESS. (Nunito Light), all caps.
  5. Headlines use the darkest color in the system.
  6. Every claim gets an asterisk, and the FDA disclaimer goes on the same piece.
  7. Use the real logo and icons. Never redraw them.

    Logo & Icons

    Logo

    Official full-color NOW logo. On dark or saturated colors, use the NOW logo with white outline. Never recolor, stretch or add effects. Clear space: at least the height of the "o".

    Icons

    Rounded 1.5-2px strokes on a 48px grid, with and without fills. In layouts they sit on a colored disc (Diet Badge, Icon Benefit). Allergen icons carry a diagonal slash or "x". Third-party marks (Non-GMO, Intertek, NPA, UL, MenaQ7, USPlus) are official files only.
