01/05

The idea

Make the testing the hero.

Supplement shoppers can't see quality on a shelf. The campaign puts NOW's testing program up front and pairs it with value, so every unit answers two questions at once: can I trust it, and is it worth it?

"At NOW, we believe your health is worth obsessing over. That's why we conduct 31,000 tests each month to ensure our supplements meet the highest standards for quality, purity and potency."

Campaign body copy
  • Quality obsessed. Priced less.
  • We don't just quality test. We quality obsess.
  • Tested obsessively. Priced ordinarily.

02/05

Promo films

Inside the lab.

Two spots take viewers into NOW's testing labs: the 31,000 number built over beakers of softgels, and a lab scientist giving the product a "passed" seal. I managed the agency that produced them.

03/05

Online video · CTV · Streaming TV

A frame that sells while the film plays.

Branded frames wrap the spots on online video and TV screens. The film plays in the open panel while the frame carries the line, the product and the call to action.

  • Shoppable online video: a product carousel cycles through the range.
  • Connected TV: a QR code takes viewers from the couch to the shop.
  • Color does the work: purple, yellow, blush and orchid versions keep a long flight fresh.

The 31,000 Quality Tests film playing in the shoppable online video frame. Press play to watch it in place.

04/05

Custom publisher carousel · Meta

Holiday obsessed? Get health obsessed, too.

For the holidays the "obsessed" idea turns inward: everyone obsesses over the perfect gift, so obsess over your own health too. A five-card publisher carousel tells it as a story, closing on Magtein Calm, and social carries the line in the campaign purple and yellow.

05/05

The system

One idea, 166 assets.

A tight kit kept a big media plan consistent: a bold two-word line, a short color set, the product bottle as the hero, and the same call to action everywhere. Each channel got the sizes and versions it needed, including holiday and Spanish-language Amazon units.
